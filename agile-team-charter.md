# Agile Team Charter

## 1. Project Overview

**Module:** PRP372S – Project Presentation  
**Project:** Laundry Pick-up and Drop-off Service  
**Class:** Group 3D  
**Charter Date:** 4 May 2026

The purpose of this project is to develop a convenient and affordable laundry pick-up and drop-off service for residents in Maitland, Observatory and Woodstock. The proposed service aims to help working adults, elderly residents and people living in apartments without laundry facilities.

## 2. Evidence of Learning

Our Agile Team Charter documents the team's purpose, vision, project goals, member roles, working agreements, communication channels, decision-making process, Definition of Done, core values, risks, constraints and project milestones.

The charter also explains how the team plans to use Agile practices, including daily stand-ups, weekly planning, reviews and retrospectives.

## 3. My Role and Contribution

My assigned role in the team was **UI/UX Designer**. My responsibilities included planning the application's screens, considering how users would navigate the application, and helping ensure that the interface would be understandable and easy to use.

This role helped me understand the importance of considering user needs before implementing the system.

## 4. Personal Reflection

Working on the Agile Team Charter helped me understand the importance of teamwork, planning, and communication in developing a successful application. As the UI/UX Designer, my responsibility was to focus on making the application simple, user-friendly, and easy to navigate. I learned that designing an application involves more than making screens look attractive; it also requires understanding users' needs and ensuring they can complete tasks without confusion.

The project helped me recognise the importance of planning the user interface and navigation before coding begins. By thinking about how users would interact with the laundry pick-up and drop-off service, I could better understand how a clear layout and logical navigation could improve the overall user experience. I also learned that the needs of different users, such as busy working adults, elderly residents, and people without laundry facilities, should be considered when designing an application.

Another important lesson was the value of having clear team roles, working agreements, and communication methods. The team charter established expectations for how we would organise our work, share information, manage challenges, and work towards our project goals. I learned that good communication and collaboration are important because design decisions can affect other parts of the system, including development and testing.

One area I want to improve is my ability to create effective wireframes, plan user journeys, and apply usability principles when designing interfaces. In future projects, I would like to gather more feedback from potential users and use it to improve my designs before development starts. Overall, this experience helped me understand the responsibilities of a UI/UX Designer and the importance of creating applications that are practical, accessible, and centred on users' needs.
